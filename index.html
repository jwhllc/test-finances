Key definitions
Chase cash ▼
Your operating checking account at Chase. Immediately spendable — no call, no approval, no wait.
Liquid reserves (MM accounts) ▼
Accounts 1005-7413 and 8847-4912 at Wells Fargo. Money market funds earning ~3.6% annually. Accessible within 1–2 business days by calling Kevin or submitting a redemption request.
Board-designated endowment ▼
Account 8805-0682. Not donor-restricted — no external legal barrier — but accessing principal requires a formal board vote. Think of it as a last-resort backstop, not operating cash.
Operating runway / months covered ▼
(Chase + MM reserves) ÷ monthly burn = how long BRYC can operate with no new revenue. Sector minimum: 3 months. NORI standard: 6 months. BRYC target: 8 months.
IPS spending policy (4% rule) ▼
BRYC's Investment Policy Statement limits annual withdrawals from the MM accounts to 4% of the 3-year average June 30 balance. FY27 authorization: ~$105K. FY26 actual draw: ~$250K — a policy exception.
Conservative vs. optimistic pipeline ▼
Conservative = lower estimate if the ask isn't fully successful. Optimistic = best case. Use conservative for budgeting; optimistic for goal-setting.
Earned YTD (investments) ▼
Actual dollars the portfolio earned year-to-date — from Kevin's updates or the WF portal (Investment Results column). Includes income and appreciation. Different from what Molly journals — her entries are IRS bookkeeping figures, not market performance.
3-month buffer (forecast) ▼
Minimum cash BRYC wants to maintain at any point. Months where projected cash drops below this are fundraising urgency windows — not a crisis, but revenue needs to close by then.
Reminders and tips
Sync buttons require internet
Pipeline data comes from Fundraising Revenue_Forecast_Planning (tab: FY27 Revenue_IMPORT). Expense data from FY27 Operating Budget tab. Won't work offline.
Data saves to this browser only
WF balances, Chase balance, advisor log entries, and pipeline data save to browser localStorage. They persist between sessions on the same device but won't appear on another machine.
Admin vs. viewer
Admin (PIN: 2025) can update balances, log advisor updates, add/delete pipeline entries. Viewer can see everything but not edit.
"This Year" in WF statements resets January 1
BRYC's fiscal year runs July–June. For fiscal year income totals, add "This Period" across July through June statements.
When to use each tab
Dashboard — daily gut check. Cash position — near-term projection. Investments — after WF statement or Kevin call. Pipeline — after sheet update. Forecast — board meetings, fundraising strategy. History — grant narratives, board context.

Using the Forecast tab for board or fundraising conversations
Owner: Josh Howard or Lucas Spielfogel▼
Owner
Josh Howard or Lucas Spielfogel
When
Before board meetings or major revenue decisions
What this is
How to model cash runway and identify fundraising urgency windows.
StepItem
1Ensure pipeline and expense syncs have been run recently. Stale data produces misleading projections.
2Click Forecast. Pick your start month and months to model.
3Set Revenue confidence to reflect how certain you are pipeline gifts will close. 100% = close everything. 80% = close 80%.
4Check the pipeline breakdown — it shows exactly what's committed, what's expected in the window, what's overdue, and what's outside the window.
5Read the "When do we need to raise?" table. Each row is a fundraising deadline month.
Note: A month in this table is not a crisis — it means revenue needs to close by then.
6Toggle Use optimistic pipeline on and off to show the range. Run both for a board presentation.
Watch out: The forecast spreads revenue evenly across the period. In reality revenue is lumpy — use the Pipeline tab filtered by quarter for a more granular view of when money is expected.
