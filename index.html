Where to see what you've earned

Log in at wellsfargoadvisors.com and select your account. Two tables to know:

Business Brokerage — return percentages

Headers: Last Month · YTD · 1Y · 3Y · 5Y · Since Inception
The YTD column = year-to-date return %. Log as "YTD return %" in the advisor update below.

Investment Results — dollar earnings

Headers: Return · Beginning Market Value · Deposits Minus Withdrawals · Investment Results · Ending Market Value
Investment Results = actual dollars the portfolio earned, net of your own cash movements. Log as "YTD earnings $."
Ending Market Value = current account value.

You don't need to wait for Kevin. Pull these three numbers from the portal anytime and log them in the advisor update form below.
