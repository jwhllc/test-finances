What is "Board-designated endowment"? BRYC's endowment account (8805-0682) is set aside by board decision — not legally restricted by donors. Think of it as a last-resort backstop, not day-to-day liquidity.

How to find the combined yield from your statement
Step 1: Open the Combined Snapshot (first few pages of your WF monthly statement).
Step 2: Find the table labeled "Combined portfolio summary."
Step 3: Look at the bottom row: Asset value = total portfolio. Far-right column = Estimated Ann. Income.
Step 4: Divide: Est. Annual Income ÷ Asset Value = blended yield. Example: $103,627 ÷ $3,172,715 = 3.27%

Important distinction: The $103K+ estimated income is a taxable income figure that reinvests automatically — it's not cash landing in your Chase account. The actual liquid income earned on just the two MM accounts (7413 + 4912) runs approximately ~$63K/fiscal year at current rates. To confirm, ask Kevin for the monthly dividend credited per account — not the taxable income Molly journals.
IPS spending policy — 4% of 3-year avg June 30 balances
What this means: Per BRYC's Investment Policy Statement, the board has set a spending rule: you may withdraw up to 4% of the average of the last three June 30 balances from the two MM operating reserve accounts (7413 + 4912). This smooths out market swings. The calculation updates automatically as you update balances above. FY26 note: ~$250K was drawn vs. ~$105K authorized — flagged as a policy exception.
