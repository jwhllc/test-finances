1Wells Fargo Advisors portal — live, updated daily
Log in at wellsfargoadvisors.com → Accounts → select account → Performance tab. Shows YTD return in dollars and % as of today. Also: Accounts → Activity for month-by-month dividend credits.

When Kevin calls or sends a report, he provides account value, YTD earnings ($), and YTD return (%). Log these below — that's what populates the Earned YTD card above. Same number as the portal, just as of the date he pulled it.
